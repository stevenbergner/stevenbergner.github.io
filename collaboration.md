# Research collaboration

I welcome focused exchanges in computational biology, scientific data analysis, and reproducible research methods. A useful first conversation usually starts with a published study, a public or shareable dataset, and a precise question that can be checked independently.

I can contribute careful data review, reproducible analysis, experimental design, and clear documentation of methods and results. I am especially interested in single-cell perturbation studies and complementary cellular measurements across models or experimental contexts.

A link or accession and a short description of the available files is enough to begin. A broad partnership commitment is not required.

I am based in Greater Vancouver and hold a PhD in Computing Science from Simon Fraser University.
